MP43247 accepted 44.1 kHz joint stereo SBC with a requested bitpool of
2–53. The 32UE reported an active A2DP stream and persisted the peer at

-50 dBFS and the test A2DP route. The 32UE then reported audio enabled.

**H5 short smoke failed.** Over 30.383 seconds, the FIFO ranged from 0 to
2,724 frames, correction reached the 500 ppm limit, and output readiness
briefly muted audio several times while A2DP remained connected. There were
zero new underrun, overrun or I2S read errors.

**H7 speaker reconnect smoke passed.** COM4 rebooted once and recovered
streaming to the saved MP43247 peer in 4.811 seconds. The -50 dBFS routed
keep-alive remained enabled on the S31, and the 32UE reported audio enabled
at the end of the capture.

The test build used the existing address-specific inquiry overlay and the
new SBC selection and PCM conversion in `d6ba7fe`. It restored the
1,400 ms heartbeat setting. The 48 kHz fallback and mono path did not
run on this speaker. The A2DP-out PCM tap currently records 48 kHz stereo
only. H5 and H7 verdicts cover these short scripted runs.

## Initial pairing attempts

The DevKitC-32UE on COM4 found MP43247 at `c9:5c:fd:6e:28:46` in all
three 30-second inquiries. It logged 295 discovery records across three
distinct addresses, including 80 records for MP43247. Each inquiry
produced one brief A2DP connection. The firmware's required 48 kHz stereo
SBC configuration failed with status 6 each time. The firmware then
dropped the link and cleared the peer. It stopped at the three-inquiry
limit, with no saved speaker bond or streaming audio.

The app-only flash of the existing diagnostic image verified on the known
COM4 adapter before the inquiries began. The same image remained in place
through the retries. BigBoy's prior speaker bond had been removed, and
Windows listed no present MP43247 device during the run. Windows returned
an error when asked to disable the BT600, so the adapter remained enabled.

The initial build never established the stable A2DP stream needed for
the keep-alive or the speaker smokes.
