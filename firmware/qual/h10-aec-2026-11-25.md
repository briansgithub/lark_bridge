## Root Causes Found & Fixed During H10 Bring-Up

1. **Internal SRAM exhaustion panic during HFP + AEC calls (`BT_OSI: malloc failed`, `reset_code=4`):**
   - `CONFIG_SPIRAM_USE_CAPS_ALLOC=y` left Bluedroid and AEC competing for ~32 KB internal SRAM.
   - **Fix:** Enabled `CONFIG_BT_ALLOCATION_FROM_SPIRAM_FIRST=y` (`firmware/s31/sdkconfig.aec-fd`), moved `s_mic_ring` (`256 KB` = 1,333 ms) and `s_aec_reference_ring` (`64 KB` = 2,048 ms) to PSRAM (`audio_router.c`), and allocated AEC engine + all 5 AEC frame buffers in PSRAM (`aec_gate.c`). Free internal SRAM during active AEC calls increased from ~30 KB to **81.0 KB** (`79.9 KB` largest contiguous block).
2. **Acoustic path delay exceeded old 200 ms / 48 KB mic ring limit:**
   - Measured end-to-end acoustic delay for `S31 I2S -> 32UE FIFO (85 ms) -> SBC/BT (~50 ms) -> MP43247 jitter buffer (~450 ms) -> Lark A1 (~30 ms)` is **~615 ms**.
   - **Fix:** Expanded PSRAM mic/reference rings and raised `CONFIG_LARKBRIDGE_S31_AEC_REFERENCE_DELAY_MS` Kconfig range to `0..950 ms`.
3. **RTOS priority inversion dropping USB ISO packets during AEC:**
   - Unpinned `aec_task` (prio 10) preempted `s31_usb` (prio 9) and `s31_uac`/`uac_host` (prio 8), pulling effective Lark UAC capture rate down from `48,038 Hz` to `47,915 Hz` (`-1,769 ppm`).
   - **Fix:** Pinned `s31_usb` (Core 0, prio 12), `uac_host` background task (Core 0, prio 11), `s31_i2s_tx` (Core 0, prio 11), `s31_uac` (Core 0, prio 10), and `s31_aec` (Core 1, prio 5). Active-call UAC rate stabilized at `48,002 Hz` with zero drops.
4. **ESP-SR 2.5.1 `AEC_MODE_FD_LOW_COST` / `AEC_MODE_FD_HIGH_PERF` fail to converge on ESP32-S31:**
   - Synthetic zero-drift noiseless loopback (`mic = 0.5 * ref[t - 16 ms]`) proved `AEC_MODE_FD_LOW_COST` (mode 5) produces only **`0.08 dB`** ERLE on `esp32s31`, and both `FD` modes (5 and 6) decay to `< 1.8 dB` on live acoustic echo.
   - Switching to **`AEC_MODE_VOIP_LOW_COST` (mode 3, 256-sample / 16 ms frames)** immediately achieved **20.24–22.29 dB** sustained ERLE with zero decay, half the frame latency (`16 ms` vs `32 ms`), `5.6–6.2 ms` peak CPU time per frame, and `81.0 KB` free internal SRAM (vs `37.7 KB` for `VOIP_HIGH_PERF`).
5. **Continuous-phase alignment & post-AEC HFP SCO output-ring clock-slip protection:**
   - Replaced stateless per-frame endpoint `bridge_resample_stereo_s16()` in `aec_gate.c` with continuous-phase Q32 `bridge_resampler_process()` driven by a 100 ms low-pass-filtered PI controller (`Kp=0.5`, `Ki=64/65536`, `±1500 ppm`).
   - Added a watermark slip absorber (`[48 ms, 128 ms]` band, 1–2 sample pad / 1-sample trim) on `s_aec_output_ring` in `audio_router.c` with `5`-frame (`80 ms`) prefill and an 8-underrun (`60 ms`) reset threshold so long-term crystal skew and transient bursts between S31 I2S (AEC producer) and remote Pixel HFP SCO TX (uplink consumer) never drain or overflow `s_aec_output_ring` or force an AEC resync.
6. **Dynamic acoustic-delay tracking for arbitrary / drifting Bluetooth sinks:**
   - Live extended Discord voice-call testing exposed that MP43247's internal A2DP jitter buffer ramps upward at ~`+2.3 ms/s` (e.g., `540 -> 652 ms`) and periodically flushes downward by ~`150 ms`. A fixed startup delay loses causal alignment whenever a sink shifts or when switching between different car head units / Bluetooth speakers.
   - **Fix:** Replaced one-shot startup mic discard in `aec_gate.c` with a 32 KB PSRAM reference delay line (`1,024 ms` capacity, `0..880 ms` range) paired with a continuous 2-stage zero-mean Pearson envelope cross-correlator (`1.15 s` window, 2 ms bins, `0..896 ms` search every `1.5 s`, `< 1 ms` CPU on Core 1). Whenever residual lag leaves `[2, 28] ms` with `corr >= 0.72` (immediate at `>= 0.80` or 2 consecutive agreeing windows), `ref_delay_samples` updates smoothly to `total_lag_ms - 12 ms` with zero ring flushes and zero resyncs.
